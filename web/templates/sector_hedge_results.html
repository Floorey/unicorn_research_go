Methodology

Prices are generated using a **Single-Factor Beta Model**. Individual stock returns are correlated to the Index based on provided Beta coefficients, with added Gaussian idiosyncratic noise ($\sigma_{idio}$).

Option Pricing

Call options are re-priced at every monthly step using the **Black-Scholes-Merton** model. Time decay (Theta) and Gamma effects are fully captured in the path dynamics.

Hedge Mechanics

The Index Short position acts as a linear hedge. Strategy performance depends on **Alpha generation** (stocks outperforming their Beta-adjusted expectations) and option convexity.
